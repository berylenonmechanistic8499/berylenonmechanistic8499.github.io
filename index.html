🙋‍♀️ Frequently Asked Questions For Beginners

Why is my conversation "too long" for AI?

AI tools have a maximum number of "tokens" (pieces of text) they can process at once. When your prompt is too long, it cuts off the beginning, so the AI forgets your early instructions. This tool makes your prompt short enough to fit, so the AI remembers everything.

Is my code safe?

Yes 100%. This is a local tool. It runs on your computer. Nothing gets uploaded to the cloud. The compressed output is what you copy and paste into your AI tool, but the original, full version stays only on your machine.

Do I need to be a developer to use it?

Not at all. It's point-and-click. If you can copy text and drag a folder, you know how to use this app.
