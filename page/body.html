    Where the data comes from

    NSE stocks and NSE indices
    Prices, delivery, P/E, P/B, dividend yield, market cap, filings and board meetings come from the National Stock Exchange's own daily reports.
    SENSEX, S&P 500, NASDAQ and Dow
    Yahoo Finance, because no official source for these is usable here. Each of them says so beside its pivot.
    Company financials
    Yahoo Finance's annual figures, labelled where they appear.
    News
    Google News, kept only from established publishers such as Reuters, Bloomberg, The Economic Times, Mint, Moneycontrol, Business Standard and CNBC.

    For information only, not investment advice. Check figures against the exchange before you trade.
